Report interpretation and data integrity

Availability reports reflect saved portal observations, not guaranteed current vacancies. Failed, cancelled or incomplete scans must not be counted as zero available beds. Protected spaces are stored independently and must not be removed or marked released by a new scan. A blank value means the data is unavailable or was not recorded.
